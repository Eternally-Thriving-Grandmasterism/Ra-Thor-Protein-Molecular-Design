# 5–10 Year Roadmap — De-Novo Protein & Molecular Design

**Goal:** Raise wet-lab success rates and deliver high-impact functional molecules (enzymes for planetary health, precision therapeutics).

## Phase 1 (2026–2028)
- Closed-loop design → expression → assay pipelines with open tools (RFdiffusion, ESM, etc.).
- Priority targets: plastic-degrading enzymes, PFAS destruction, robust carbon-capture catalysts.

## Phase 2–3
- Industrial-reliability success rates.
- Personalized therapeutic binders with rapid feedback.
- Integration with bioprinting / molecular reports already in parent lattice.

TOLC 8 + PATSAGi governance throughout.
