# Phase 1 Concrete Questions (Closed-Loop Reliability)

1. What closed-loop protocols (design → filter → express → assay → feedback) currently deliver the highest experimental success rate per sequence tested for enzymes and binders?
2. Can RFdiffusion2-style active-site scaffolding be combined with property guidance (solubility, expression, stability) to raise hit rates further?
3. Robust plastic-degrading enzyme designs that retain activity under real environmental temperature, pH, and contaminant conditions.
4. Carbon-capture catalysts with practical turnover numbers and operational stability.
5. Minimal experimental rounds needed to surpass directed-evolution baselines on therapeutically relevant targets.
